What it does

It remembers what matters.

Tell Nova once that you don't take calls before ten, or that Sam is vegetarian. It keeps that in mind every time it plans for you.

It acts, then reports back.

Rescheduling, drafting replies, booking tables, setting reminders. You get a short record of each, never a mystery.
