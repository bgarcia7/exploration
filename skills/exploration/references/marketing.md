# Marketing

Covers positioning, messaging, campaign concepts, ad angles and creative, launch plans, channel experiments, landing-page angles, email sequences, social content series, and growth experiments.

## What the seed is for here

Marketing ideas gather around the same few moves: name the pain, list three benefits, add social proof. The seed pushes concepts into angles and audiences you wouldn't try first. Results decide what wins, so every concept should come with a cheap way to test it. Make **three concepts by default**, since comparing and testing them is the point.

## Fixed vs open

**Usually fixed:** what the product actually does, its price and availability; claims that would need evidence; ad-platform and legal policy; brand guidelines; budget; and the target market if the user gave one.

**Usually open:** which audience segment to focus on, the core insight or tension, the angle, the emotional register, the kind of proof, the format, the hook structure, how the offer is framed, and the call to action. The channel is open only if the user didn't fix it.

The seed never licenses invented statistics, fake testimonials, false scarcity, or claims the product can't back up. Honesty is a fixed requirement here. A concept that only works if it stretches the truth gets dropped.

## Reading the seed

Index for structure and read associatively for tone. Walk the digit sequence, one digit per menu. Then let the letter runs and the case rhythm set the voice: a hard, uppercase-heavy seed suggests punchy and declarative; a soft lowercase one suggests intimate and conversational.

**Angle**
0 pain first (name the problem precisely) · 1 identity and aspiration (who you become) · 2 an enemy (the status quo or the category villain) · 3 a contrarian claim · 4 proof and data led · 5 the story of one user · 6 the product moment, shown rather than told · 7 humor or absurdity · 8 ritual and habit · 9 timeliness (only if something real is happening)

**Audience moment**
0 first-time switcher · 1 power user of a competitor · 2 the skeptic · 3 a lapsed user · 4 buying for someone else · 5 has tried everything · 6 early adopter and tinkerer · 7 overwhelmed · 8 budget-conscious · 9 status-seeking

**Format**
0 short video (hook in the first two seconds) · 1 a single image and a headline · 2 carousel · 3 founder letter · 4 testimonial-style (real customers only) · 5 comparison chart · 6 interactive tool or quiz · 7 a riff on a meme or trending format · 8 long-form essay or thread · 9 stunt or event

## Building

For each concept:

- **Insight:** one line on the truth about the audience that this concept uses.
- **Hook or headline**, then the body copy or script.
- **Visual direction:** read `design.md` or `media.md` if it needs more than a line. To show the concept's key visual, generate it with fal by following `fal.md`: a key image, a short video spot, or a music sting. Never generate fake customers, testimonials or proof.
- **Call to action** and where the concept runs.
- **Test:** the cheapest way to learn whether it works, the metric to watch, and what result would kill it.

Explain any jargon you use, because the reader may not be a marketer.

## Deliverable

The concepts side by side, followed by a short recommendation on which to test first and how to read the result.
