# Media: images, video, and audio

Covers prompts for image, video, and music generators; art direction; storyboards and shot lists; video concepts; photography briefs; and sound design.

## Fixed vs open

**Usually fixed:** the subject, brand assets, aspect ratio and duration, platform specs, rights (no real people's likenesses and no other brands without permission), and the budget or credits for generation tools.

**Usually open:** medium and technique, lighting, palette, lens and framing, era or reference point, composition, editing pace, music genre and tempo, and texture.

## Reading the seed

Index the craft choices from the digit sequence. Read the rest associatively:

- **Palette:** hex-like runs, used as in `design.md`.
- **Tempo:** map the digit sum into 60–180 BPM, or use it as the shot count.
- **Scenes:** the digital root sets the number of scenes or beats.
- **Cutting rhythm:** run lengths suggest shot lengths, with long runs as held shots and breaks as cuts.

**Medium**
0 35mm film photograph · 1 risograph print · 2 clay-style 3D render · 3 watercolor · 4 collage · 5 technical diagram · 6 stop-motion · 7 infrared or thermal look · 8 woodcut or linocut · 9 low-resolution pixel art

**Lighting**
0 hard midday sun · 1 golden hour · 2 soft overcast · 3 neon night · 4 one practical lamp · 5 backlit silhouette · 6 on-camera flash · 7 candle or firelight · 8 bright studio · 9 underwater caustics

**Framing**
0 extreme wide · 1 wide · 2 medium · 3 close-up · 4 macro · 5 top-down · 6 low angle looking up · 7 tilted · 8 over the shoulder · 9 dead-center symmetry

## Building

- **Prompts:** make them specific enough to reproduce the result. Cover subject, medium, lighting, framing, palette, mood, and what to avoid.
- **Anything with several shots:** a storyboard table with a row per shot (number, duration, framing, action, audio).
- **Generation:** generate images, video, music, sound effects, voice and 3D with the fal MCP by following `fal.md`, which covers model choice (highest quality by default), prompting each medium, and inspection. If fal isn't connected, hand over prompts that are ready to run.

## Deliverable

Prompts or a storyboard for each variant, plus generated media if it was approved. The seed never appears in prompts that get saved or shared, or in the final media.
